Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our short-let services, bookings, and policies.

Booking & Reservation

You can make reservations by contacting us directly via WhatsApp (+234 903 499 6825), Instagram (@cnhomes.shortlets), or through our social media channels. We'll guide you through the booking process and confirm availability.

Check-in time is 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional charges.

Yes, we require a refundable security deposit for all bookings. This deposit is held during your stay and returned within 24 hours after check-out, provided there's no damage to the property or missing items.

Accommodation & Amenities

All rooms include unlimited Wi-Fi, smart TV, PS5 console, quality bedding, towels, toiletries, and basic kitchen essentials. Additional amenities vary by room type and selected electricity package.

Yes, all our suites include fully equipped kitchens with refrigerator, microwave, cooking utensils, cutlery, and dinnerware. Some units also feature electric stoves and other appliances depending on the electricity package selected.

For stays of 5 nights or more, complimentary mid-stay cleaning is provided. For shorter stays, additional cleaning services are available upon request at an extra charge.

Policies & Payments

Cancellations made 7 days or more before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 3-6 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in are non-refundable. Please contact us for any special circumstances.

We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and cash payments. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking, with the balance due before check-in.

Yes, subject to availability. Please notify us at least 24 hours before your scheduled check-out to request an extension. Additional charges will apply based on the room rate for the extended period.

Location & Services

Yes, we provide complimentary secured parking for our guests. Each booking includes one parking space. Additional parking may be available upon request.

Our property is designed for comfortable stays and small gatherings. Loud parties and events are not permitted to ensure a peaceful environment for all guests. Small celebrations may be allowed with prior approval.

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at this time due to property policies and consideration for other guests who may have allergies.
